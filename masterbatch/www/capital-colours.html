How the factory runs — Lab → Buy → Make → Sell

The lab creates the recipe; the rest of the flow turns it into shipped colour.

1 · BUY

Get raw materials in
  • Supplier — who we buy from
  • Purchase Order — what we order
  • Material Receipt — goods arrive
  • Stock increases automatically

2 · MAKE

Turn resin into colour
  • Lab Formulation — the recipe
  • Production BOM — recipe for the floor
  • Batch Production Sheet — what we made
  • QC — Passed / Failed

3 · SELL

Ship to customers
  • Customer — who buys
  • Sales Order — what they ordered
  • Delivery — goods out
  • Invoice — get paid
